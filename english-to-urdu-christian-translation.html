Frequently Asked Questions

Do you translate Christian books from English into Urdu?

Yes. Urdu Christian Translation discusses English-to-Urdu projects for Christian books, devotionals, Bible studies, theological resources, sermons, and ministry materials.

Who can enquire about a translation project?

Christian authors, publishers, churches, and ministries internationally are welcome to enquire. We can discuss the material, intended audience, project scope, schedule, review process, and budget before confirming a project.

Can you translate sermons and digital Christian content?

Depending on the project, we can discuss sermons, teaching materials, articles, website content, podcasts, and other Christian media intended for Urdu-speaking audiences.

How do I request a quote?

Email a short description of your material, approximate word count or page count, intended use, and preferred deadline. Please also confirm that you have the necessary rights or permission to translate the material.
