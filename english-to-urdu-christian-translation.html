Urdu Christian Translation

English-to-Urdu Christian Translation

A translation partner for Christian authors, publishers, churches, and ministries seeking to make trusted resources accessible to Urdu-speaking readers worldwide.

Urdu Christian Translation welcomes enquiries from international Christian authors, publishers, churches, and ministries interested in English-to-Urdu translation. Our goal is to help make Christian teaching accessible to Urdu-speaking audiences while preserving the original author’s intended message, context, and theological meaning. We can discuss project scope, permissions, editorial review, schedules, and costs before work begins.

Christian Books

Translation of Christian books, manuscripts, devotionals, and ministry publications, with attention to the author's meaning and context.

Bible Study & Theology

Christian teaching resources and theological material translated with care for biblical concepts and established Christian terminology.

Sermons & Teaching

Translation of sermons, Bible teaching, conference messages, and other spoken or written ministry resources.

Digital & Audio-Visual Content

Projects may include Christian articles, podcasts, websites, and Urdu adaptation or dubbing of audio-visual content, depending on project requirements.
