Project Process

From First Enquiry to Urdu Translation

A transparent starting point for Christian authors, publishers, churches, and ministries considering translation into Urdu. Each project is discussed individually, and the detailed scope is agreed before work begins.

The best process depends on the resource, its intended readers, rights arrangements, and the level of review required. These steps provide a practical framework for discussing a potential project; exact deliverables, dates, and costs are agreed with the client.

STEP 01

Initial enquiry

Share the resource type, original language, approximate word count or duration, intended Urdu-speaking audience, and your goals for the project.

STEP 02

Rights and permissions

Before translation or publication of copyrighted material, confirm who controls the relevant rights and obtain appropriate written permission or a licence. Agree any restrictions on format, territory, print quantity, and distribution.

STEP 03

Scope and quotation

Discuss the material to be translated, deliverables, schedule, budget, terminology preferences, and any formatting or publishing needs. Confirm the agreed scope in writing.

STEP 04

Translation

Translate with attention to the source author's intended meaning, natural Urdu expression, biblical references, and theological terminology. Any project-specific terminology guidance can be discussed in advance.

STEP 05

Review and feedback

Agree in advance whether editorial review, theological review, back-translation, or author/publisher feedback is needed, who will provide it, and how revisions will be handled. These services are not assumed unless agreed.

STEP 06

Approval and delivery

Confirm the final file format, approval process, credit lines, copyright notices, and any publication or distribution conditions before the translated resource is released.

What to send with your enquiry

If available, include the project title, author or rights holder, resource type, approximate length, target audience, intended use, desired timeline, and any existing rights or licensing information. A short description is enough to start a conversation.

Important project principles

We aim to communicate the source meaning clearly and handle Christian terminology carefully. Rights, costs, timelines, review responsibilities, and publication permissions should be agreed openly. No translation or publication of copyrighted material should proceed without the appropriate authorization.
