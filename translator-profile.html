About Pastor Salas John

Pastor Salas John is a Christian minister based in Pakistan. His work includes church ministry, Bible study, and outreach through The Will of the Father Organization, a registered Christian ministry.

He is also involved in Urdu Christian Translation, a separate initiative focused on making Christian books and resources accessible to Urdu-speaking readers through English-to-Urdu translation.

Urdu Christian Translation

Urdu Christian Translation is a separate translation initiative that aims to help Urdu-speaking Christians access Christian literature and biblical teaching in Urdu.

The work emphasizes clear language, careful handling of Christian terminology, and respect for the original author's intended meaning. New translation and publication projects should proceed with written permission from the relevant rights holder.
