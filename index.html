Our Purpose

Faithful Words. Meaningful Literature.

Serving Urdu-speaking readers through careful and thoughtful Christian translation.

Making Christian literature accessible.

Urdu Christian Translation exists to make valuable Christian books and teaching available to Urdu-speaking readers in language that is clear, natural, accurate, and faithful to the author's intended meaning.

Our goal is not simply to translate words, but to communicate the message of each work with care while respecting sound Christian theological terminology.

Our Approach

How We Translate

Translation requires more than replacing words. It requires understanding meaning, context, language, and theology.

01

Faithfulness

We seek to preserve the author's meaning without adding to or removing from the original message.

02

Natural Urdu

We aim for Urdu that reads naturally, clearly, and beautifully rather than sounding like a word-for-word translation.

03

Theological Care

Christian theological terminology is handled carefully and consistently so that important concepts retain their intended meaning.
