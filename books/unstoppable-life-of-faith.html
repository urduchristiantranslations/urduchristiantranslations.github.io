At a Glance
Based on 32 Parables of Jesus Christ

About the Book

Unstoppable Life of Faith is based on 32 parables of Jesus Christ. The author explores each parable within its historical context, helping readers understand the circumstances, meaning, and message behind Jesus' teaching.


Through these parables, the book presents a thoughtful approach to understanding and applying God's will in everyday life. It invites readers to look beyond the words of the parables and discover the principles of faith they reveal.

Exploring the Parables

  • 32 parables of Jesus Christ are explored.
  • Each parable is considered within its historical context.
  • The meaning and message of each teaching are explained.
  • The book encourages thoughtful application of God's will.
  • The parables are presented as a guide for understanding principles of faith.
