About the Book

Christology presents a comprehensive study of Christ, exploring the different aspects of His person and work. The book examines Christ's pre-existence, His birth, His earthly ministry, His death and the cross, and His resurrection.


It also explores Christ's roles and work for the Church today, together with the hope of His second coming. Through these themes, the author provides a broad exploration of who Christ is, what He has accomplished, and His continuing significance for the Church.

Exploring Christ

  • Christ's pre-existence
  • His birth and coming into the world
  • His earthly life and ministry
  • His death and the cross
  • His resurrection
  • His roles and work for the Church today
  • The hope and significance of His second coming
  • The broader person and work of Christ
