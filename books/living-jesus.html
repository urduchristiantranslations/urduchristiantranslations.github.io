Original Author: Chris Needs

Urdu Translator: Salas John

About the Book

What if following Jesus was simpler than you thought? What if the secret to a transformed life wasn't found in religious complexity, but in simply living like Him?


In Living Jesus, Chris Needs explores practical ways we can walk as Christ walked, serving, forgiving, and trusting. The book challenges cultural Christianity, calling believers back to the raw, simple, and powerful faith that Jesus modeled.


Whether you are a seasoned believer or simply exploring faith, this book offers a fresh and practical approach to truly living Jesus, not just believing in Him.

What You'll Discover

  • How to love people even when it is difficult.
  • How forgiveness can free you from bitterness.
  • How to live humbly in a world that glorifies self.
  • How prayer can become your lifeline.
  • How to overcome fear and cultivate faith.
  • How to embrace discipleship in everyday life.
  • How to handle opposition with grace and truth.
  • How to move from passive Christianity toward active faith.
